The project

A meeting point for the Italian VR community.

VRC Italia Network nasce per rendere più semplice scoprire le community italiane attive su VRChat, conoscere nuovi gruppi e dare visibilità alle realtà che contribuiscono ogni giorno alla scena italiana.

The portal does not replace individual communities: it connects them and makes them easier to discover.

VRC Italia Network Group Represent the Italian community around the world with pride.

All Italian users are invited to join the shared group, show their nationality and represent the Italian community together across VRChat.

Join VRC Italia Network

Bring your community into the network.

To keep the project useful and well organized, communities wishing to join must meet a few basic requirements.

01
Predominantly Italian community

More than 80% of the members must be Italian and more than 80% of events must be held in Italian.

02
Active presence on VRChat

The community must have opened at least 5 VRChat lobbies within the last 7 days.

03
Verifiable contacts

At least one verifiable official channel must be available, such as Discord, Instagram or a website.

04
Environment compatible with the project

The community's environment and management must be compatible with the purpose of the VRC Italia Network project.
