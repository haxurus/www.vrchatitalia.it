The project

A meeting point for the Italian VR community.

VRChat Italia nasce per rendere più semplice scoprire le community italiane attive su VRChat, conoscere nuovi gruppi e dare visibilità alle realtà che contribuiscono ogni giorno alla scena italiana.

The portal does not replace individual communities: it connects them and makes them easier to discover.

VRChat Italia Group Represent the Italian community around the world with pride.

All Italian users are invited to join the shared group, show their nationality and represent the Italian community together across VRChat.

Join VRChat Italia

Bring your community into the network.

To keep the project useful and well organized, communities wishing to join must meet a few basic requirements.

01
Italian community

The community should primarily serve Italian users or have a significant Italian presence.

02
Active presence on VRChat

It must organize activities, events, meetups or projects directly connected to VRChat.

03
Verifiable contacts

At least one contact person and an official public or verifiable space must be available.

04
Compatible with the project

Applications will be reviewed before a community is published on the portal.
